Analysis

The question

Three questions drive the work: is a movie going to succeed commercially, which features correlate most with that success, and how much can metadata alone predict revenue?

Success is defined plainly — a film returns 2.5× its production budget. The decision this supports is a greenlight call, which means the people reading it are studios, producers, and actors.

Data

  • Roughly 15,000 movies spanning 1925–2027, plus about 530,000 people, pulled from the TMDB pipeline.
  • Revenue and budget are heavily skewed, so targets get a log1p transform before modelling.
  • Only features known at decision time are allowed into the primary feature set: budget, release date, genre, production company, cast, director, keywords.

The limitations are written down rather than hidden: marketing spend is invisible in this data, older films carry unadjusted inflation, indie breakouts are rare events no model will catch, and word-of-mouth moves opening weekends.

Feature design

Modelling starts from a deliberate baseline — budget, runtime, release year and month, genre, country, language — then compares candidate feature families against it one at a time:

  • Release timing — quarter, day of week, calendar position.
  • Genre shape — primary genre, genre count, individual genre flags.
  • Production — company and country counts, primary studio and country.
  • Director & cast history — prior film count, previous average revenue, budget, and ROI carried forward from the pipeline's own tables.
  • Crew weight, keywords, franchise — crew size; flags like superhero, sequel, remake, based-on-novel; collection membership and franchise track record.
  • Certification & engagement — rating bands, plus popularity and vote signals that only exist after release, kept separate from decision-time features.

Outcome

  • A feature catalogue expressed in code and SQL — feature_engineering.py, extract_movie_features.sql, and EDA.sql — with the extracted matrix checked into the repo as extract_movie_features.csv.
  • A clean separation between features you can know on greenlight day and signals that only arrive after release, which keeps the prediction honest.
  • The work is ongoing: candidate families are still being compared against the baseline before any accuracy claim is worth printing here.
