# Portfolio landing page

## Scope

`index.html` — a single static personal-portfolio landing page for GitHub Pages.

## Visitor mode

Experience

## Audience and task

Potential collaborators, clients, and hiring managers scan a small selection of placeholder work, understand the maker's approach, and choose to make contact.

## Content and constraints

All identity, work titles, metrics, and links are placeholders. No photographs, claims, or generated images are used. The build must be static, responsive, and dependency-free.

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** A portfolio as a quiet gallery of working ideas, refusing the typical tiled-card developer résumé.

**OWN-WORLD:** Soft mineral white, near-black ink, mist blue, and a restrained electric-lime signal; generous canvas margins, hairline separators, rounded object frames, and a luminous abstract placeholder built in CSS.

**STORY:** The visitor first meets an identity and point of view, then moves through selected placeholder work, a concise practice statement, and one uncomplicated invitation to connect.

**FORM:** Quiet gallery / contemporary industrial-design catalogue; seed key `2b9c3fbd`. Signature interaction: the focal placeholder and project panels respond to pointer position with restrained depth, while content is fully visible without motion.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Memorable moment

The hero’s abstract “work in progress” object slowly shifts its light and shadow with the pointer, making the page feel considered without needing a photo.
